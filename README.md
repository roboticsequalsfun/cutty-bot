# Cutty-bot 2.0 (Even though this is the only version)

This marvel of engineering ~cuts bananas~ slices various cylindrical delicacies!

It was designed by a younger me who thought what the house needed mostly was a banana-cutting robot (I was obsessed with useless machines).

And boy… is it bad at it.

It takes around 30 seconds to 1 minute to cut a banana in half, and it can barely grip anything shorter than a banana (It struggles even at gripping _a_ banana).

Nevertheless, it’s kind of cool. Good luck trying to replicate this though — LEGO Mindstorms is discontinued, and the app is no longer easily downloadable.

I’m putting this up anyway as a small relic of the internet. Not like anyone is going to memorialize it though...

---

# How to use

It’s simple. Okay, that’s a lie. Here are the steps (don’t cry if you get stuck — I did too):

1. You need LEGO Mindstorms kit, the app (have fun with that), and something to view the pdf (if you don't have that, how have you been living?).
2. Assemble it using the included instructions (they’re not great).
3. Connect the battery (not your phone) to the computer (not a potato).
4. Upload the `.ims` file and insert the battery again (good luck).
5. Try to get it to actually cut a banana (this is where your journery ends).
6. Enjoy your weird bot that mostly just struggles with bananas.
